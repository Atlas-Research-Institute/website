What the fellowship weeks look like

  1. Week 1

    Your question

    Pick an education access problem where you live and turn it into a question you can actually answer.

  2. Week 2

    Sources

    Find published research on it and learn to tell reliable sources from weak ones.

  3. Weeks 3 to 4

    Evidence

    Work with real data and connect it back to your question.

  4. Weeks 5 to 6

    Your paper

    Write it up, get feedback on your draft, and hand in a finished paper.

Where your paper goes

Finished papers can be shared in the Atlas Journal. Atlas editors read every paper before it goes up. The journal is not externally peer reviewed.

Strong papers can also be submitted to peer-reviewed journals. Acceptance is the journal's decision, not ours.

  • International Journal of High School Research
    Peer-reviewed submission route.
  • The Curieux Review
    Student research writing partner.
  • Lumiere Education
    Program partner. Scholarships to Lumiere programs for Atlas fellows.
